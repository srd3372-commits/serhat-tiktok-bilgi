Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

Overview

SERHAT is a personal desktop workflow used by its owner to prepare original short-form video content for connected social media accounts.

Information processed

When the account owner chooses to use connected services, SERHAT may process video files, scripts, captions and the authorization data needed to complete the requested action.

Storage and sharing

Content is processed on the owner's computer and may be sent only to the connected services selected by the owner. SERHAT does not sell personal information or share it with unrelated third parties.

Security

The owner is responsible for protecting access to the computer, connected accounts and authorization credentials used with SERHAT.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the application owner through the official SERHAT repository.
