SERHAT

Video Automation for Original Content

SERHAT is a personal desktop workflow that helps its owner create, review and prepare original short-form video content for sharing on connected social media accounts.

How it works

The workflow turns an approved topic into a script, voiceover, captions and a vertical video. The account owner reviews the result and controls the sharing settings for each connected platform.

Our commitment

SERHAT is designed for original, informative and educational content. It does not collect, sell or share personal information with third parties.
